## Pass 1 — Composition

### Defect findings

None.

The softened octagonal landscape slab retains a continuous shell band around both inset harbors, balanced upper shoulder rail, low utility bridge, and deliberate central breathing room. Portrait remains a true recomposition: plum upper deck, charcoal lower deck, left structural spine, short cross-axis waist, and right utility bay divide the tall field without a dead middle.

The revision-2 cradle fully separates the system and action roles. The four-action harbor now has one continuous lower boundary with chamfered corners; the old center action notch is gone. The cradle is shell-indigo, centered below Y, and visually tied to the system index rather than the plum group. No control, ring, harbor edge, seam, shoulder face, or shell rim visibly collides. Outer silhouettes are fully contained, the landscape wells retain inset margins, portrait shoulders remain seated on the spine, and safe clearances survive all states without clipping or near-tangent artifacts.

## Pass 2 — Material and craft

### Defect findings

None.

Lighting remains consistently upper-left with compact lower-right bevel/shadow logic. The five depth tiers remain legible in both appearances: canvas, satin shell, inset wells/rails, raised native faces, and flush legends. Pressed and disabled reductions do not erase the dark-mode shell rim or harbor transitions.

The sheet-3 material revision is visible rather than token-only. Action faces use a concentrated lacquer highlight along the upper-left edge plus a crisp lower-right bevel; movement faces use a wider, softer elastomer lift. The distinction persists when color is removed. Utility, system, and shoulder keys remain appropriately lower-profile and do not borrow the action sheen.

No bloom, banding, raster seam, broken outline, shell clipping, doubled legend, inconsistent radius, coarse texture, or stray registration tick is visible at full resolution. The violet calibration seam remains subordinate, interrupted around native controls, and confined to its intended index role outside active outlines.

## Pass 3 — Interaction

### Defect findings

None.

All semantic roles preserve four distinct states in portrait and landscape, light and dark. Normal is raised and material-forward; pressed is darker, flatter, and visibly recessed; active restores the raised silhouette with crisp local violet indexing; disabled removes sheen and saturation while preserving labels and grouping. No state introduces frame drift, legend jump, scale-origin shift, or artwork movement.

The revision-2 system active ring is complete in both portrait active panels, including the upper arc that A interrupted in sheet 2. Its cradle remains visible in normal, pressed, active, and disabled, so role separation is not state-dependent. Disabled portrait Start/Select now remain comfortably legible while still subordinate to enabled controls. Native controls, labels, and state feedback remain visible throughout, with no contact-sheet evidence of an unresolved contrast or accessibility risk.

None. No optional taste preference is being converted into a defect or used to qualify the pass.
