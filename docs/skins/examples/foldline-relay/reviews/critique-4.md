## Pass 1 — Composition

**No composition defects found.**

The lower-left control cluster remains the immediate focal mass in all 16 panels and outweighs the Relay Fold by well over the required 3:1 apparent-area relationship at 25%. Landscape retains three readable stepped shoulders, separated row fields, and an uneven Escape/Palette rail. Portrait retains its own oblique crown, tall route, and staggered inner contour rather than rotating or stretching the landscape construction. The upper/right fields remain intentional proofing space; the route explains them without filling them.

### Lower-clearance reassessment

The exact landscape source panels are 492 × 226 px reductions of the 874 × 402 artboard. The native canonical row-four frames end about **5.5 artboard px** before the boundary, above the required **4 artboard px** allowance, and assigned materials add no cast-shadow footprint. At full resolution, every rectangular and circular lower contour closes normally; none is flat-cut, tangent, or visibly crowded against the boundary.

Terminal-row inspection was separated from visible-control inspection. Periodic one-pixel paper/fiber variations and near-canvas resampling values occur across the folio/canvas and are not control silhouettes. In the active landscape source row, the remaining control-aligned samples differ from canvas by only about 1–2 RGB levels and have no visible edge, face, or shadow contour. Treating those passive/resampling samples as a clearance failure would contradict both the canonical geometry and the rendered appearance. C1 is therefore closed with no replacement defect.

The previously resolved route alignment, bracket visibility, stepped silhouette, dark-plane separation, and negative-space balance show no regression.

## Pass 2 — Material and craft

**No material or craft defects found.**

Light continues to read from the upper-left: fold highlights sit upper-left, score/contact edges resolve lower-right, and no contradictory glow or spotlight gradient appears. Light and dark remain separately authored rather than inverted. The dark canvas, lower score field, raised plane, and native controls retain distinct depth tiers at full resolution and 25%.

Native faces remain matte and nearly flat. Crisp cut/ply edges preserve elevation even though the constrained row-four materials omit cast shadows. That zero-shadow choice is a successful boundary tradeoff in the rendered evidence, not a flattening defect: normal still reads raised, while pressed relies on centered scale, uniform darkening, and inset/edge change. No seam, clipped curve, fold-layer contradiction, texture blob, duplicate legend, or raster artifact is visible.

`Command` now has the same functional authority as the other long legends. In the exact landscape panel its primary cap raster spans five source rows (consistent with the ≥9 artboard-px target after reduction), remains one line, and leaves roughly four source pixels on each side—more than the required 4 artboard-px clearance. Portrait also remains single-line with clear side space. `Return`, `Prefix`, `Escape`, and `Palette` remain untruncated and readable in every state and appearance.

## Pass 3 — Interaction

**No interaction defects found.**

- **Normal** establishes the strongest material identity and stable geometry.
- **Pressed** is center-anchored, smaller, uniformly darker, and lower without legend movement or a new gradient direction. The dark system pill retains a complete traceable silhouette.
- **Active** returns to full geometry, preserves every assigned face color, and adds one crisp short top-edge registration dash. The dashes are visibly absent in normal, do not enclose controls, and retain the intended chartreuse/ultramarine/dense-ink role logic.
- **Disabled** reduces chroma, flattens depth, quiets edges, and preserves understandable grouping and legend contrast rather than applying opacity alone.

State changes do not shift layout. Native faces, labels, and system controls remain visible; no artwork substitutes for controls or creates a fake tappable target. The four states are meaningfully distinct side by side in both appearances and orientations.
