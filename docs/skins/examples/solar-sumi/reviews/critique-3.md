# Pass 1 — Composition

No composition defect remains.

The landscape silhouette retains meaningful asymmetry after reduction rather than relying on full-resolution bevel detail. Its joystick island, narrow current neck, and open action channels form a balanced left-to-right grouping without recreating a case or unified button plate. The outer deck edge remains clear of B4/B8.

Portrait remains a genuine recomposition: the broad action crown, mostly quiet left waist, right-side bend, and lower joystick bay establish an upward path. The top fiber cap and side channels remain open in all states. Neither orientation has a clipped shell edge, unexplained void, crowding regression, or control/artwork tangent.

# Pass 2 — Material and craft

No material or craft defect remains.

Dark mode preserves separate canvas, smoked deck, absorbed current, native control, and legend tiers at both inspection scales. Light and dark are not simple inversions. The current stays optically flat while deck and controls retain coherent upper-left highlights and lower-right containment. Fiber texture remains subordinate and directional.

The pass-2 mechanical notch rhythm has been removed: portrait rails no longer carry three evenly spaced pale cuts, and landscape action edges no longer create a second register parallel to the gold marks. Carbon edges read as irregular authored masses without becoming noisy. Labels remain centered, upright, and unwarped. No seam, halo, banding, accidental clipping, duplicate legend, or contradictory light direction was found.

# Pass 3 — Interaction

No interaction defect remains.

- **Normal:** stable raised baseline with the intended vermilion, carbon-soft, carbon, and fiber families.
- **Pressed:** controls darken and flatten with reduced shadow while centers and labels remain fixed.
- **Active:** raised geometry returns; high-contrast perimeter/fill treatment is visible across the action and utility groups. Masking or ignoring the joystick does not collapse active toward normal at 25%.
- **Disabled:** every role loses saturation, specular weight, and cast depth. The four disabled panels are unmistakably unavailable in a headerless 25% comparison, while labels and semantic grouping remain legible.

No native frame, center, or legend visibly translates between states. The active treatment is stronger than normal and distinct from pressed; disabled no longer reintroduces an energized puck or assertive vermilion.

## Originality and trade-dress check

The original signatures remain the asymmetric Offset Fiber Deck, the connected Carbon Current, and sparse gold Solar Register. The current revision does not show a manufacturer mark, model name, logo, proprietary glyph, cabinet outline, familiar commercial case, mounting plate or bolt pattern, circular action plate, copied row framing, game art, duplicate artwork label, ensō/open circle, rising-sun device, seal block, kanji, pseudo-calligraphy, or another prohibited cultural shorthand.

The filled joystick island is irregular, materially connected to the current, and visibly unlike a hardware mounting plate. The action geometry follows the canonical contract without adding commercial trade-dress cues.

## Defects versus optional taste

No criterion-linked defect or unresolved major finding remains. The aggressive portrait waist cut and broad gold energy in the active state are authored taste choices; neither weakens control hierarchy, state identification, clearance, or originality in the rendered evidence.
